FAQs

Are telemedicine consultations available?

Yes. Video consultation is available for several specialties, including Neurology, Dermatology, Oncology, Pulmonology and Nephrology.

Do you accept health insurance?

Yes. We work with cashless insurance partners and accept PM-JAY (Ayushman Bharat). Contact our team to confirm coverage for your plan.

How much does a consultation cost?

Consultation fees vary by specialist. Contact us or book an appointment to get the current fee.
